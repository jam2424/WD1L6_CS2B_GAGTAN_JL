My design philosophy

I design like I'm making a sticker: flat shapes, strong outlines, and soft pink and purple tones that make a page feel friendly before anyone reads a word. Looking at my three projects, three habits show up every time.

Software can be useful and still be fun. My Sakatos Shop uses cartoon characters, and GroceryKo uses a shop awning and rounded shapes. People relax around soft shapes and warm color, so they stay longer and click with confidence.

I pick a small palette and give each color a role. Hot pink marks the main action, light purple marks small details, and deep purple anchors the page. When every color means something, I don't need extra labels to explain the page.

Good interfaces answer every click. The Show Grade Equivalent button reveals a new table, and a checked grocery item updates the progress bar. I use JavaScript so the page always shows the result of what the user just did.
